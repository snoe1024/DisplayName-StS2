[b]Client-side mod: no longer affects gameplay![/b]

[b]Your display name is used everywhere in multiplayer.[/b]
Your chosen display name is shown as your player name instead of your Steam username.
Leave it blank to keep your Steam username.

[b]When other players don't have this mod[/b]
If the host has RitsuLib, players with this mod share their display names.
Players without this mod see your Steam username.
Without RitsuLib on the host, only your own display name on your own screen changes.

[b]Branch support[/b]
Supports both the default and Public Beta branches.

[b]Requires[/b]
RitsuLib (installed automatically as a Workshop dependency).
[b]To make this mod optional for other players, its dependency has changed from BaseLib.[/b]
If the mod doesn't load properly after updating, check that RitsuLib is installed.
